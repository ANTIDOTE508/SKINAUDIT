What changed and why

  1. Responsive layout New

    On desktop, picking sits on the left and details on the right, so choices and answers are visible together. Below 1024px it becomes one column: pick first, details underneath. The footer is always pinned.

  2. Treatment-room backdrop New

    The photo sits behind frosted panels. It's dimmed more on phones, where text covers most of the image, and less on desktop, where the chair shows on the left.

  3. Pre-filled areas New

    Most tools and treatments start on the usual areas (full face for facials, steamers and LED; forehead, nose and chin for a pore vacuum). A note tells them it's pre-filled and invites a change. "Looks right" confirms it in one tap. Treatments that vary a lot (fillers, surgery, Other) start empty.

  4. Split by place

    An At home / In clinic toggle shows 15 or 20 options, never all 35. Badges count the picks on the hidden tab.

  5. Details as a checklist

    Each pick becomes one row with a progress ring and a one-line summary. One row is open at a time, and it moves to the next one when finished.

  6. Skip what's implied

    Answering Daily or Weekly hides "When did you last have it?"

  7. Name your "Other"

    Other asks "What is it?" first, uses the name as the row title, and offers to switch if it matches an option already listed.
