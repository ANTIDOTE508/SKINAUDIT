How It Works

Your routine, understood in context.

SkinAudit brings the different parts of your skincare routine into one place, helping you see how they work together and how your regimen evolves over time.

You bring the routine.

SkinAudit brings the perspective.

01

Build your Dossier.

Start with what you already use.

Add your skincare products, tools and treatments to create a structured picture of your current routine.

No need to start over.

No need to buy something new.

Your existing regimen is the starting point.

02

Map your Ritual.

Skincare isn't only about what you use.

How a routine comes together can change the picture. Organize your morning and evening rituals so SkinAudit can understand your regimen as a whole.

Because the routine matters as much as the product.

03

Add Context.

There is always more happening around a routine than what sits on the shelf.

SkinAudit brings that wider picture into view.

Because what surrounds a routine can matter as much as what's in it.

04

See your Audit.

Once the picture comes together, SkinAudit surfaces insights across your regimen.

See areas worth paying attention to, understand where your routine may deserve a closer look, and gain a clearer view of how its different parts relate.

Not another product rating.

A view of the system around it.

05

Follow what changes.

A routine is not a one-time decision.

Products change. Routines evolve. Treatments happen. Your skin responds. SkinAudit lets you check in over time, creating a record that makes it easier to recognize what has changed alongside your regimen.

One audit gives you a snapshot.

Time gives it meaning.

The Principle

Clarity you can act on.

SkinAudit turns the complexity of your regimen into guidance you can actually use. Understand what is working together, what may deserve reconsideration, and where there may be room for something different.

So when your routine needs to change, you're not left guessing what to keep, what to adjust, or what to consider next.

A routine that makes more sense — and guidance for where to go next.
