Philosophy

Skincare was never just a list of products.

Your skin experiences a routine as a system.

Products overlap. Ingredients interact. Treatments intervene. Weather changes. Habits shift. And what worked six months ago may not make sense today.

SkinAudit was built around a simple idea:

Understanding the relationship between these things matters as much as understanding the things themselves.

The Problem

We have more skincare information than ever. And less clarity about what to do with it.

Most skincare tools begin with a product.

Is this ingredient good?

Is this product compatible with my skin type?

What should I buy next?

SkinAudit begins somewhere else:

What is already happening in your regimen?

Because a product can make sense on its own and still make less sense within the system surrounding it.

Skin is
not static.

It responds to repetition, environment, treatments, behavior and time. That means understanding a routine requires context.

Not simply

What are you using?

But

What are you using, together, under what conditions, and what happened next?

Our Approach

01

Relationships over ingredients

An ingredient tells you something. Its relationship to everything around it tells you more.

02

Context over absolutes

There are very few universally “good” or “bad” skincare decisions. Frequency, tolerance, environment, treatments and the rest of the regimen matter.

03

Observation over assumption

SkinAudit helps you record what changes instead of forcing every experience into a predefined skin-type label.

04

Intelligence over instruction

The goal is not to tell you what your skin should do. It is to make the information surrounding your decisions easier to understand.

The Shift

From product literacy to regimen literacy.

For years, skincare education has taught us to recognize ingredients, concentrations, claims and categories.

That knowledge still matters.

But knowing what is inside a bottle is different from understanding what happens when that bottle becomes part of a routine.

SkinAudit is designed for the second question.

The Next Step

Your routine is already producing information. SkinAudit helps you see it.
